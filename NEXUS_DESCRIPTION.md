# Nexus Mods description

## Short description

```text
Hire mercenary clans directly from the Kingdom screen. Set contracts and strategic priorities, track performance, and manage loyalty.
```

## Main description (Nexus BBCode)

```bbcode
[b]Hire mercenary clans quickly—then keep them worth the investment.[/b]

Hiring mercenaries in vanilla Bannerlord means searching the campaign map for a clan leader, reaching them in time, and hoping the clan does not leave shortly afterwards.

[b]Mercenary Management puts the entire hiring process into the Kingdom screen.[/b]

Browse available mercenary clans, compare them, and hire one without personally tracking down its leader somewhere across Calradia.

Once hired, the mod adds contracts, strategic priorities, performance tracking, and retention mechanics—giving you the information and tools needed to manage that relationship.

[b]Fast and convenient hiring[/b]

[list]
[*]Browse available mercenary clans from one interface.
[*]Hire a clan without finding and speaking to its leader.
[*]Compare military strength, party count, reputation, expected price, and acceptance chance.
[*]See clans already employed by other kingdoms.
[*]Manage every active mercenary contract from the same screen.
[/list]

No more chasing mercenary leaders around the map just to reinforce your kingdom.

[b]Manage active contracts[/b]

After hiring a clan, you can:

[list]
[*]Set an individual payment rate between 85% and 150% of the native rate.
[*]Choose a 30, 60, or 120-day contract.
[*]Create an indefinite contract or one that lasts until a selected war ends.
[*]Assign a strategic priority.
[*]Monitor satisfaction, reliability, contribution, and performance.
[*]See the risk of departure or employer termination.
[*]Revise, renew, or terminate the contract.
[/list]

Payment, relationships, losses, performance, military conditions, and the assigned role all affect contract decisions. Better terms improve retention, but no contract guarantees permanent loyalty.

[b]Strategic priorities[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Independent Operations[/b] — neutral behavior close to native Bannerlord AI.
[*][b]Join Armies[/b] — encourages participation in friendly armies.
[*][b]Economic Warfare[/b] — favors raids and attacks against economic targets.
[*][b]Territorial Defense[/b] — favors settlement defense and nearby threat removal.
[/list]

Priorities influence the decisions already considered by Bannerlord's AI. They do not issue direct orders or disable recovery, recruitment, resupply, and self-preservation.

[b]Playing as a mercenary[/b]

A player-led independent clan can:

[list]
[*]Compare offers from eligible kingdoms.
[*]Review payment, wars, danger, duration, and the proposed role.
[*]Offer its services to a kingdom.
[*]Accept or reject calculated offers.
[*]Inspect an active contract and expected payment.
[*]Request improved terms or end its service.
[/list]

AI kingdoms also use the contract system. They evaluate their military situation, available budget, clan strength, relationships, and reliability when hiring, retaining, renewing, or dismissing mercenaries.

[b]Compatibility[/b]

[list]
[*]Single-player only.
[*]Made for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord [b]1.4.7[/b].
[*]Requires compatible versions of Bannerlord.Harmony and Bannerlord.UIExtenderEx.
[*]Compatibility with other Bannerlord versions is not currently guaranteed.
[/list]

[b]Installation[/b]

[list=1]
[*]Install compatible versions of [b]Bannerlord.Harmony[/b] and [b]Bannerlord.UIExtenderEx[/b].
[*]Install with your mod manager or extract the [b]MercenaryManagement[/b] folder into the game's [b]Modules[/b] directory.
[*]Place both dependencies above Mercenary Management in the launcher.
[*]Enable the mod.
[/list]

Creating a separate campaign save before installing or updating is recommended.

[b]Описание на русском[/b]

[b]Главная цель Mercenary Management — сделать найм наёмников быстрым и удобным.[/b] Больше не нужно бегать по карте в поисках лидера нужного клана: доступных наёмников можно просматривать, сравнивать и нанимать прямо через интерфейс королевства.

После найма вы можете установить ставку и срок службы, назначить стратегический приоритет, следить за результативностью, удовлетворённостью и надёжностью клана, а также оценивать риск его ухода. Контракт можно пересмотреть, продлить или прекратить. Независимый клан игрока также может сравнивать предложения королевств, предлагать свои услуги и управлять собственной службой.

[b]Мод поддерживает русскую локализацию из коробки — отдельный русификатор не требуется.[/b]
```
